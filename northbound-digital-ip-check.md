## 2. Australia

### 2.1 Company / business name (ASIC) — **confirmed record**
- **Entity:** NORTHBOUND DIGITAL PTY LTD
- **ACN:** 642 903 008
- **Status:** ASIC published a **Notice of Proposed Deregistration** under s 601AB(3) of the
  *Corporations Act 2001*, published **~16 April 2024**.
- **Interpretation:** s 601AB(3) deregistration is typically ASIC-initiated (e.g. for an outstanding
  annual review fee or apparent ceasing of business). Deregistration usually completes roughly two
  months after the notice, so as of this report (June 2026) the company is **most likely already
  deregistered**. **[VERIFY current status]** on ASIC Connect.
- **Implication:** If deregistered, the company name itself frees up, but (a) the prior use can still
  found a passing-off / Australian Consumer Law claim by the former operators if they retain
  goodwill, and (b) deregistration says nothing about any trade mark they may hold.

*Source: ASIC Published Notices — Northbound Digital Pty Ltd 642 903 008 (Proposed Deregistration).*

## 6. Recommended next steps (and where to verify)

Do these before adopting/registering the name:

1. **Run the official trade-mark searches** (per class, ideally classes 35 & 42 plus any others):
   - IPONZ Trade Mark Check — https://app.iponz.govt.nz/app/TradeMarkCheck
   - IP Australia Trade Mark Search — https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/
2. **Confirm company/business-name status:**
   - NZ Companies Register — https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/search/
   - NZ Business Number (NZBN) — https://www.nzbn.govt.nz/
   - ASIC company search (confirm whether Northbound Digital Pty Ltd 642 903 008 is now deregistered)
     — https://connectonline.asic.gov.au/ and ABN Lookup — https://abr.business.gov.au/
3. **Check domain availability** for `.co.nz`, `.nz`, `.com.au`, `.au` and any `.com`/`.digital`
   you'd want (note the offshore holders above).
4. **Consider a TM-Headstart / pre-filing assessment** with IP Australia and/or a NZ–AU trade-mark
   attorney for a binding clearance opinion and to file in the right classes — "Digital" alone is
   weakly distinctive, so protect the distinctive element ("Northbound") and/or a logo.
5. **If clear, file early** in both countries (NZ via IPONZ; AU via IP Australia, or use the Madrid
   Protocol for multi-country filing) to secure priority.
